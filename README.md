# Data Deception and Legal Boundaries

A static educational project about defensive data handling, canary records, and the legal and ethical boundaries of data deception.

## Important: nothing here runs

This repository is deliberately non-executable:

- It contains no scripts, services, dependencies, or setup instructions.
- It makes no network requests and does not interact with any system.
- Its examples are fictional static text and JSON for discussion only.
- Do not put real credentials, personal information, or confidential data in examples.

The word “simulation” here means reading and discussing a fictional scenario. It does not mean running a technical simulator.

## Topics

- Containing a suspected data exposure
- Revoking or rotating credentials through authorized processes
- Using clearly fictional canary records in systems you control
- Preserving evidence and documenting decisions
- Distinguishing defensive measures on your own systems from actions directed at external systems

## Scope and disclaimer

This project is for general education, not legal advice or incident-response advice. Laws and obligations vary by jurisdiction, contract, industry, and facts. Consult qualified counsel and your organization’s security and privacy teams before acting in a real incident.

The examples do not establish that any particular measure is lawful. A measure confined to systems you control may still raise privacy, employment, contractual, regulatory, or evidence-handling issues.
